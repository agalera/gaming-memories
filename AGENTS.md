## Website

`site/` is the published site, and `site/docs.html` is its documentation. A
user-facing change belongs there as well as in the README.

Write it in the voice of the sections around it — short declarative sentences —
and reuse the existing `doc-heading`, `doc-steps`, `doc-callout` and
`table-wrap` classes rather than adding new ones. Sections are numbered, so one
inserted in the middle renumbers those after it, and every section needs an
entry in both the sidebar nav and the "On this page" list.
